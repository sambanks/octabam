# STEM REC: eight tracks under the emulator

Piece 2 of the roadmap in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-stem-rec-upstream-port-design.md`,
section 0. Piece 1, the port, is merged into `main` (`ed486b3`).

## 1. Purpose

Prove under the emulator that STEM REC records all eight tracks
correctly and continuously, before the menu (piece 3) and the first flash
(piece 4). Three things the roadmap names:

- A fixture with sound in every frame. Today's fixture sounds for its first
  four frames only, so a lost or repeated sector in the silent rest of a
  take can't be seen.
- Eight-track takes that stream, wrap and fill the ring, at every track
  count.
- The peak ring fill, kept by the recorder, for the menu's status row and
  for measuring the card on the unit.

## 2. Decisions (Yves, 27 Sep 2026)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Which tracks the build records by default | All eight (`stems_tracks = 0xFF`), as the roadmap's "settings reset at boot: all 8 on". Piece 3's menu turns tracks off. |
| Use the emulator's card speed to find where the ring runs out | Yes: a sweep of track counts against card speeds (section 6). |
| How the fixture gets sound into eight tracks | THRU machines fed a four-channel test WAV through `--audio-in` (section 4). |

## 3. The module

- **Default mask.** `stems_tracks` becomes `0xFF`. At eight tracks a ring
  frame is 512 bytes and the 4 MiB ring holds 8,192 frames, about 3 s.
- **Peak ring fill.** A new word, `stems_peak`, a global symbol: the largest
  number of frames in the ring the hook has seen in the take. The hook
  already computes the frames in the ring every recording frame, for its
  overflow guard; it keeps the largest value. Arming resets it to 0, and it
  keeps its value after the take ends. A share of the ring is
  `stems_peak / stems_rframes`, computed by the reader, never by the hook.
- **Cost.** About 3 or 4 more instructions per recording frame. The hook's
  cost is measured again with the method of `docs/firmware/STEM_REC.md`
  12.2, at 1 and 8 tracks.
- Nothing else changes: the streaming, the file names, the header, the
  60-minute cap, the regions.

## 4. The fixture

- **`stems_fixture.py --thru`** writes `out/stems_fixture_thru.json` and its
  card, from the same project template as the other fixtures:
  - all eight tracks THRU machines, in every part of banks 1 and 2 (the
    port's load can play either bank's part);
  - a trig on step 1 of every pattern for every track, so each THRU track
    starts with play;
  - each track on its own mix of inputs: T1 A, T2 B, T3 C, T4 D, T5 A+B,
    T6 C+D, T7 A+C, T8 B+D. If two tracks still carry equal signals, levels
    separate them.
- **The input.** A four-channel WAV of independent seeded noise at −12 dBFS,
  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, 30 s, generated by the fixture into `out/test_audio/`.
  Ours, never Elektron's. Noise doesn't repeat, so a take can only match
  its track at the true offset. `ot_emu --audio-in` feeds it to inputs A to
  D from the transport start.
- **Measured first.** Which part-record bytes select a THRU track's inputs,
  and whether the amp envelope must be held open. `tools/hw/ot_project.py`
  names an INAB byte at part offset `+0x3f` and marks THRU input routing as
  unproven. The first task of the plan measures both under the port before
  anything depends on them.
- **The fixture's proof.** Under the port, every track's read-back sounds in
  every frame once play starts, and the eight read-back signals are
  distinct. If the input routing can't be made to work, the fallback is the
  port's built-in tones, and that change is raised with Yves before it is
  made, because tones repeat and weaken the offset check.
- The existing fixtures stay as they are: the one-track FLEX card, the
  eight-track FLEX card and the FAT32 card, with their checks.

## 5. The checks

In `tools/verify/verify_stems.py`, which `make check REMIX=stems` runs as
STEM REC's gate.

- **Today's checks keep their meaning.** Each run that assumes T1 only
  pokes `stems_tracks = 0x01` before play. A new static check requires the
  build's default to be `0xFF`.
- **One take per mask**, on the THRU fixture: armed before play, streaming
  for at least three chunks (512 frames each), stopped, then given the time
  the writer needs at the emulator's card speed. For each take:
  - each file equals its own track's read-back at one fixed offset, sample
    for sample;
  - every frame of each file has sound from its first frame with a
    non-zero sample on (no all-zero frame after the track starts);
  - no two files are alike;
  - the writer wrote during the take, and ended IDLE with no error;
  - `stems_peak` equals the largest ring fill rebuilt from the port's
    watch log of `stems_wr` and `stems_rd`, exactly.
- **Masks in `make check`:** 1, 2, 4 and 8 tracks (the first n), and the
  sparse mask `0xA5` (T1, T3, T6, T8).
- **`--long` adds** 3, 5, 6 and 7 tracks, and two long runs at eight
  tracks:
  - **wrap:** a take past the ring's 8,192 frames, whose files must equal
    the read-back;
  - **overflow:** the ring made to look nearly full, as today's overflow
    check does; the guard trips, and eight complete files are written and
    closed.
- **The fixture's proof** (section 4) is a check too: every track sounds in
  every frame, eight distinct signals.

## 6. The card-speed sweep

- **`tools/verify/stems_sweep.py`**, run by hand, not in `make check`: 1, 2,
  4 and 8 tracks, each at a card delay (`ot_emu --ata-latency`) of 8, 16,
  24, 32, 48 and 64 samples per sector. 8 is the emulator's default, about
  2.2 MB/s effective (`docs/firmware/STEM_REC.md` 13.3).
- **Each run** is a 5-second take on the THRU fixture, stopped, then drained.
  It reports: the peak fill, in frames and as a share of the ring; whether
  the take overflowed, and when; the writer's measured rate; and, when the
  fill is still rising at the end, its growth rate and the predicted time
  to overflow.
- **The result** is a table of which card speeds each track count survives
  with the 4 MiB ring. About 2 to 3 hours of runs, once.
- **Its limit.** The emulated card has a constant delay per sector; a real
  card stalls. OctaLab measured single card writes of up to 1.2 s while
  STATIC tracks play, and 1.4 to 2.1 MB/s while they read (their
  `docs/OTX_PROJECT_PROPOSAL.md`, 26 Sep 2026). At eight tracks STEM REC
  needs 1.41 MB/s, and a 1.2 s stall needs about 1.7 MB of ring, inside the
  4 MiB (about 3 s) the ring holds. Those two figures are arithmetic, not
  measurements. The sweep gives steady-rate limits; flash A measures the
  real card.

## 8. Rules

- `stems.s` changes only as section 3 says. Every existing check still
  passes.
- Every value that moves is a finding: re-measured, explained and written
  down, never loosened to pass.
- Every gate runs on a committed tree, and its log's first line shows it.
- No Elektron byte in the repository. The WAV and every card image stay in
  `out/`.
- Nothing is pushed.

## 9. Risks

- **The THRU routing may not work under the port.** Measured first (section
  4); the fallback needs Yves's approval.
- **Two tracks may carry equal signals** after their input mixes. The
  fixture's proof catches it; levels separate them.
- **`make check REMIX=stems` grows** by about 15 to 20 minutes (an estimate
  from 27 Sep's runs), and `--long` by more.
- **The emulated card isn't the unit's card** (section 6).
- **The input starts at the transport start.** Frames recorded between the
  arm and the transport start are silent, so "every frame has sound" is
  checked from the frame each track starts.

## 10. Done when

- The build's default mask is `0xFF`; `stems_peak` resets at the arm and
  keeps the take's largest fill; the hook's cost is measured at 1 and 8
  tracks.
- The THRU fixture's proof passes: eight tracks sound in every frame, eight
  distinct signals.
- `make check REMIX=stems` passes, with the new checks, on a committed tree.
- `verify_stems.py stems --long` passes, with the new checks.
- The sweep has run, and its table is in `STEM_REC.md` section 15.
- The READMEs and the manifest's text say all eight tracks.
