## What it does

Keeps the audio editor's zoom when you listen to slices. Normally, playing a slice from the trigs in SLICES trig mode zooms the editor out to show the whole slice, so a close-up of a marker is lost every time. With ZOOM KEEP the zoom stays where you set it and the editor just scrolls to the slice.

## How to use it

Nothing to set. Zoom in on the SLICE page (encoder F), then play slices from the trigs in SLICES trig mode: the zoom stays and each slice's start scrolls into view.
