## What it does

Scene locks and the crossfader for page 2 of FX1 and FX2. Stock scenes
lock page 1 only; the page-2 knobs (slots 6–11) had no scene lock.

## How to use it

Open an FX1 or FX2 page 2, hold scene A or B and turn a knob on slots
6–11: the lock goes to the held scene. Hold FUNC while turning to remove
the lock. With a scene held, the page-2 dials show that scene's lock, as
the page-1 dials do.

- A scene holds eight page-2 locks across its tracks (128 a Part); a
  scene with all eight taken drops a turn that would add a ninth.
- Scene copy, paste, clear and undo carry the page-2 locks with the
  rest of the scene.
- With PLOCKS P2 in the remix and no scene held, held trigs show the
  first held step's page-2 lock on the dial.
- A knob press with a scene held toggles the page-1 lock of that knob
  (stock); page 2 uses FUNC+turn to remove a lock instead.

## Notes
