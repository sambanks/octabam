This module adds scene locks and crossfader modulation for page 2 (slots 6–11)
of FX1 and FX2. On stock firmware, scenes can only lock page-1 parameters.

## Operation

Open page 2 of FX1 or FX2 (press FX1 or FX2 twice to open its SETUP window),
hold Scene A or B, and turn a knob on slots 6–11 to assign a lock to the held
scene. Hold FUNC while turning the knob to clear the lock. While a scene is
held, the page-2 dials display that scene's locked values, just like page 1.

- Each scene supports up to eight page-2 locks across all tracks (128 per
  Part). Once all eight slots in a scene are full, turning another knob will
  not add a ninth lock.
- Scene copy, paste, clear, and undo include page-2 locks along with the rest
  of the scene.
- When PLOCKS P2 is included in the remix and no scene is held, holding a trig
  displays the first held step's page-2 lock on the dial.
- Pressing a knob while holding a scene toggles that knob's page-1 lock (stock
  behavior); use FUNC+turn to remove a page-2 lock instead.
