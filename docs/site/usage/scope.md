SCOPE displays track audio full-screen on the LCD in three modes: **OSC** (a
mono waveform polyline), **XY** (an L×R Lissajous vectorscope), and **ALL** (a
4×2 grid of mini oscilloscopes with T1–T4 on the left, T5–T8 on the right, a
centre divider, and T1 at the top-left, matching the track button layout). OSC
and XY also show a gain/peak HUD and a `!` clip indicator.

Press **`[DOWN]` + `[TEMPO]`** to open SCOPE (**`[UP]` + `[TEMPO]`** opens
[TUNER](tuner.md)). Press **`[UP]`** or **`[DOWN]`** to cycle through the
three modes. Turn encoder **A** to adjust GAIN and encoder **B** to adjust
TIME. Press encoder **A** to reset GAIN and clear the peak indicator.
Pressing and releasing **`[CUE]`** toggles the gain/peak HUD in OSC and XY
modes (ALL mode has no HUD).

Opening SCOPE does not extend the brief silence that occurs when an effect
loads.
