While the sequencer plays, the tempo LED flashes green on the pattern's
first step and red on every other beat, as stock does. A visual downbeat
for when the click track is not an option. Nothing to set.

## Operation

Press PLAY and watch the tempo LED: the first flash is green, then red
until the pattern comes round again. The green follows the pattern's
length: at 12 or 20 steps it still lands on the first step. Stopped, the
LED behaves as stock, and the next PLAY starts green again.

## Notes

- The tempo LED only flashes on whole beats. When the pattern's first step
  falls between beats (14 steps, say), the green comes on the first flash
  after it.
- Projects, Parts and settings are untouched.
