While the sequencer is playing, this module flashes the tempo LED green on the first step of each pattern and red on every other beat, giving you a visual downbeat without needing a click track. It works automatically with no settings to configure.

## Operation

Press PLAY to see the indicator: the tempo LED flashes green on step 1, then red on subsequent beats until the pattern loops. The green flash adapts to the pattern length, so it still lands on the first step of 12-step or 20-step patterns. When the sequencer is stopped, the LED behaves as on stock OS, and pressing PLAY again starts with a green flash.

## Notes

- Because the tempo LED only flashes on whole beats, patterns whose first step falls between beats (such as a 14-step pattern) show the green flash on the next whole beat.
- Projects, Parts, and system settings are not affected.
