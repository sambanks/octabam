## What it does

A SCALE row in PROJECT > CONTROL > SEQUENCER (OFF, then 24 scales), a
ROOT row under it (C .. B) and a GLIDE row (OFF, 1..127).

## What they change

- The PTCH knob, parameter locks and CHROMATIC trig keys snap to the
  scale built on the root.
- Key 1 of the CHROMATIC keyboard sounds the root.
- A sample track's keyboard plays the one complete root-to-root octave.
- GLIDE is the slide time of the synth's and a sample track's legato
  (the LEG box on AMP SETUP).
- Polyphonic chromatic keys on a synth track whose VOIC is 2..4.
- The three bytes survive a power cycle.
