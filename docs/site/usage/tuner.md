## What it does

A guitar-tuner readout of the current audio track: the note, the octave,
a ±50 cent needle and the frequency, from the track's post-FX pre-fader
audio.

## How to use it

Hold UP and press TEMPO: the window opens. TEMPO, YES, NO or the chord
close it. TEMPO alone, FUNC + TEMPO and UP alone stay stock. Until it
has heard a steady note the window shows `--`.
