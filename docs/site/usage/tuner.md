TUNER displays a chromatic tuner readout for the current audio track, showing
the note name, octave, a ±50 cent needle, and the frequency measured from the
track's post-FX, pre-fader audio.

## Operation

Hold [UP] and press [TEMPO] to open the tuner window. Press [TEMPO], [YES],
[NO], or [UP] + [TEMPO] again to close it. [TEMPO] alone, [FUNC] + [TEMPO], and
[UP] alone keep their stock behavior. Until the tuner detects a steady pitch,
the readout shows `--`.
