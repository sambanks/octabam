The shared MIDI trig-mode menu keeps installed playing modes together.
Hold **FUNC** and use **UP/DOWN** to choose a mode. With both chord modules
selected, **CHORDS** plays Factory's absolute chord slots and **CHORD PLAY**
plays Harmony's scale-degree roots and chord variations.

![CHORDS and CHORD PLAY in one menu](../../img/trig-modes/combined.png)

Use Factory on HARM OFF tracks. Use Harmony on other tracks for progressions
that change with KEY. Each module keeps its own recording and held-note
behavior; switching modes releases the notes owned by the previous mode.
Native TRACKS, CHROMATIC and QUICK MUTE remain available. Audio-track modes
keep their native meanings.
