Ties the bus delay to the project: the delay follows the project tempo
(the tempo itself is stock's), and its TIME knob reads as a tempo division
(`1/8`, say) while it sits on one, in milliseconds otherwise.

## Operation

There is no menu: it works wherever the bus delay runs. A MIDI note held
on the delay track's channel reaches the delay: in GRAIN mode it sets the
grains' pitch in place of PTCH.
