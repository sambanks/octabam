MODULATION is a multi-mode modulation effect offering Juno chorus, Dimension
chorus, a through-zero flanger, a tuned comb filter, and a phaser, plus a
LOFI stage. MIX 0 is an exact dry passthrough and MIX 127 is 100% wet.

## Access

Press FX1 or FX2 on a track and select MODULATION from the effect chooser
alongside the stock effects.

## Operation

- MODE on page 2 selects the effect: JUNO, DIM, FLNG, COMB, or PHSR.
  Switching modes resets the knobs to that mode's defaults.
- RATE sets the LFO speed (0.08–10 Hz), and DPTH sets the sweep depth around
  DLY.
- DLY sets the centre delay time (0.2–23 ms). In FLNG it becomes MANL
  (manual delay), in COMB it sets pitch (44 Hz to 5.5 kHz, exponential), and
  in PHSR it becomes STGS (2, 4, 6, or 8 stages).
- FDBK controls feedback. In PHSR it sets phaser regeneration, and in COMB
  it sets decay and polarity.
- LOFI degrades the signal using sample-and-hold (1–64 samples, down to
  690 Hz) and bit reduction below 24 bits for values above 64. It sits on the
  delay line in JUNO, DIM, FLNG, and COMB, and on the wet output in PHSR.
- TONE is a lowpass filter on the delay line (modeling a BBD); in COMB it
  controls brightness and is labeled BRIT.
- WDTH sets the right channel's LFO phase lag: 0 is mono, 64 is quadrature,
  and 127 is antiphase.
- DSP load: with MODULATION active on all eight tracks, the effects processor
  runs at about 26 % at default settings and up to 85 % at its most demanding
  settings (an empty project reads about 24 %). Exceeding 100 % stalls the
  unit until you power-cycle it.

## Parameters

| Page | Knob | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RATE | 0–127 | 26 | LFO speed 0.08–10 Hz (26 = 0.5 Hz); no LFO in COMB |
| 1 | DPTH | 0–127 | 21 | sweep depth; LFO range in PHSR |
| 1 | DLY | 0–127 | 18 | centre delay 0.2–23 ms; MANL in FLNG; pitch in COMB; STGS in PHSR |
| 1 | FDBK | bipolar | 64 | feedback; regen in PHSR; decay and polarity in COMB |
| 1 | LOFI | 0–127 | 0 | sample hold 1–64 samples and bit reduction; 0 is off |
| 1 | MIX | 0–127 | 0 | dry/wet; 127 is wet only |
| 2 | MODE | JUNO / DIM / FLNG / COMB / PHSR | — | selects the effect |
| 2 | TONE | 0–127 | 80 | lowpass on the delay line (127 is an exact bypass); BRIT in COMB |
| 2 | WDTH | 0–127 | 127 | right LFO lag: 0 mono, 64 quadrature, 127 antiphase |
