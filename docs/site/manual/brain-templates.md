*BRAIN: Templates*

Templates store reusable setups on the CF card. Press **LEFT / RIGHT** to switch
between the **KIT**, **MIDI**, and **PROJECT** tabs.

1. Select **NEW** and press **YES** to create a template.
2. Enter a name and confirm with **YES**.
3. Select a saved template to view its actions and values.
4. Choose **APPLY TO…** to load it into your current setup, or **SET AS
   DEFAULT** to use it automatically as a starting point. **CLEAR DEFAULT**
   removes the default assignment.

You can also **SAVE**, **RENAME**, and **DELETE** templates from the template
screen.

- **KIT** templates store a complete Part and can be applied selectively by
  section and track: FX, LFO, SRC, AMP, MIDI TRACKS, SCENES, and MACHINES.
- **MIDI** templates store the parameter pages of a single MIDI track.
- **PROJECT** templates store the stock PROJECT, CONTROL, and MIDI menu
  settings. Setting one as default applies it to every new project.

KIT templates leave sample slot assignments and track levels unchanged, and they
cover the SRC and AMP pages of all machine types. Because each Kit corresponds
to a saved Part, applying a KIT template updates the current Part. When applying
a KIT template, you can apply the entire Part, a single section across tracks, or
copy between a specific source and destination track. A default KIT template is
used for Part Clear and for the Parts in new projects.

Use **LEFT / RIGHT** or the encoder to adjust values. Press **NO** to go back;
if you have unsaved edits, you are prompted with **SAVE CHANGES?**. Duplicate
template names are not allowed.

## Page shortcut

To save or load the current parameter page directly, hold a page key (SRC, AMP,
LFO, FX1, or FX2) and press **FUNC**. This opens a menu with **SAVE FX
DEFAULT**, **CLEAR FX DEFAULT**, **SAVE TEMPLATE**, **LOAD TEMPLATE**, and
**DELETE TEMPLATE** (plus **NAME KNOB** on MIDI tracks). On FX1 and FX2 pages,
only **SAVE FX DEFAULT** and **CLEAR FX DEFAULT** appear, because effects do not
use templates. On an MKII, **FUNC** plus pressing the **LEVEL** encoder opens
**TOOLS > LOAD**.

**SAVE FX DEFAULT** saves the current FX page settings as the default whenever
that effect is selected in the future, and **CLEAR FX DEFAULT** removes the
saved default. **SAVE PROJECT** updates the backup copy on the card.

On a MIDI track, **NAME KNOB** lets you assign a custom label (up to four
characters) to each of the ten assignable CCs on CTRL1 and CTRL2. Entering an
empty name clears the label.
