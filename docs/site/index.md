# Octabam

*A love letter to the Octatrack*

Octabam adds community-built features to the Elektron Octatrack's operating
system (OS 1.40C), including Kits, templates, expanded scenes and parameter
locks, USB audio and MIDI, and new effects. You build it in your browser from
your own copy of OS 1.40C and install it from a CF card or over MIDI. This site
covers **version 0.5** and its three remixes: [Octabam](remix-octabam.md),
[Octabam Verbs](remix-octabam-verbs.md), and
[Octabam Bus](remix-octabam-bus.md). Octabam is unofficial community software;
see [About](about.md).

## Unofficial firmware

Octabam is unofficial firmware and is not made, endorsed, or supported by
Elektron. Installing it is generally safe, and you can revert to stock OS at
any time ([Safety and Recovery](safety.md)). Elektron is not responsible for any
damage to your Octatrack.

## Get started

1. Choose a remix: [Octabam](remix-octabam.md) keeps all stock effects and adds
   new ones, [Octabam Verbs](remix-octabam-verbs.md) replaces the stock reverbs,
   and [Octabam Bus](remix-octabam-bus.md) adds a shared delay and reverb bus.
2. [Build and install](web-builder.md) the image.
3. Read the [Octabam Manual](reference/manual/index.md), which covers all three
   remixes and notes which remixes include each feature.

## Everyday use

- Save templates or change defaults: [BRAIN](reference/manual/brain/index.md)
- Assign a saved sound setup to a pattern: [Kits and patterns](reference/manual/parts/kits.md)
- Lock parameters on an effect's second page: [Page 2 parameter locks](reference/manual/parts/plocks-p2.md)
- Control MIDI tracks with scenes: [MIDI scenes](reference/manual/parts/midi-scenes.md)
- Record MAIN and CUE on a computer: [USB audio and MIDI](reference/manual/midi-usb/usb.md)
- Look up any effect or feature: the [manual](reference/manual/index.md)

## Reading this manual

This manual covers what Octabam adds to stock OS and assumes you are already
familiar with tracks, Parts, patterns, scenes, and the front panel. **Bold
labels** refer to controls and menu items. Arrows show menu paths, such as
**PROJ → BRAIN → TEMPLATES**. A plus sign indicates a key combination: hold
**FUNC** while pressing the other key. Commands and filenames appear in
`monospace`. Screen illustrations are captured from the firmware with example
values.
