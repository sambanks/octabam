# `xfcurve` — XF CURVE

The crossfader's curve, chosen in PROJ > BRAIN > SETTINGS: **LINEAR** (stock), four S-curves
(**S SOFT**, **S-CURVE**, **S FIRM**, **S HARD**), their inverses (**INV SOFT**, **INV-CURVE**,
**INV FIRM**, **INV HARD**), **CUT**, **CUT A**, **CUT B**, **SLOW A** or **SLOW B**.

| CURVE | the share of scene A, u = fader travel toward A (0..1) | for |
|---|---|---|
| LINEAR | u | stock, bit for bit |
| S SOFT, S-CURVE, S FIRM, S HARD | a normalized logistic, slope at the middle 1.25, 1.5, 2 and 3 × stock's | the scenes hold near both ends and the move gathers in the middle, more so down the list |
| INV SOFT, INV-CURVE, INV FIRM, INV HARD | the inverse functions of those four: slope at the middle 0.8, 0.67, 0.5 and 0.33 × stock's | fine control around the blend; the fader leaves each scene quickly (INV HARD's first step is 12% of the way) |
| CUT | 0 up to u 0.45, 1 from 0.55, linear between | the fader as a scene switch, with a short blend so XVOL and levels do not step |
| CUT A, CUT B | scene B (CUT A) or A (CUT B) everywhere but the last steps at that end: one step of dead travel, then a three-step ramp (0.25, 0.5, 0.75) | the fader as a momentary, scratch-style cut: lock a track's XVOL off in one scene and the fader cuts it in and out at that end; tracks without scene locks keep playing |
| SLOW A | 1 − (1 − u)² | fine control near scene A |
| SLOW B | u² | fine control near scene B |

The curve reaches everything the crossfader reaches on the audio tracks: page-1 scene locks,
XVOL, the STRT/LEN/RATE morph, SCENES P2's page-2 locks and MORPH's tracks (MORPH reads the same
table). A change in the menu applies at the next crossfader move.

## How it works

Stock turns the fader position xf (0..127, 127 = scene A) into scene weights through one table
of 128 signed shorts at `0x400bcd90`: `w[x] = -round(32768 x / 127)`, scene A's share in Q15,
negated. Nine reads in the image (`lea 0x400bcd90`) all index it by the position `0x460d16c8`;
the two that matter every frame are the crossfader handlers' rebuilds of the per-track weight
longs `0x80003c60..` (panel `0x40061e40`, MIDI CC 48 `0x400626ba`), which the frame builder's
scene morph and XVOL read. So the module only rewrites the table.

Both handlers' `lea 0x80003c60,%a0` (`0x40061e34`, `0x400626ae`) become a `jsr xfc_hook`. When
CURVE differs from the curve last written, the hook copies that curve's 256 bytes over the stock
table, then replays the `lea`. LINEAR's table is generated by stock's formula and equals the
image's bytes (the gate checks it), so choosing LINEAR again restores stock exactly. Nothing runs
per frame. The curves are generated in `manifest.py` (`CURVES`).

## Limits

- **MIDI tracks stay linear.** MIDI SCENES mixes them from the raw position with its own linear
  weight (`modules/midi-scenes/scenes.s`, `mix`).
- **A CURVE change waits for the fader.** Until the next move the table and the weights keep the
  previous curve.
- **CARD scope**: one curve for every project on the card; no per-track or per-Part curve yet.
- Select parameters (MODE, WAVE...) still jump where the blend crosses the middle; with CUT that
  is the middle of the fader, as stock.

## Proof

On hardware: Bryan's MKII, 6 Nov 2026, in his everyday remix (`_bt_mtrx_io_vib`: BRAIN, STORE,
KITS, MIDI SCENES, SCENES P2, REC MATRIX, USB IN/OUT, among others). Every curve chosen in BRAIN and
swept with the panel crossfader, CUT A / CUT B as a scratch-style cut on a track's XVOL.

`tools/verify/verify_xfcurve.py` under the port: panel crossfader moves with CURVE at LINEAR
(never written), CUT, CUT then LINEAR again, S-CURVE and CUT A, set through BRAIN's value table;
the pinned build (`remixes/test/xfcurve-scurve`). It also passes on the `octabam` remix plus XF
CURVE (with MORPH, MIDI SCENES, SCENES P2, STORE, BRAIN).

Not covered: the MIDI CC 48 crossfader (the same routine from the second site; no gate or
hardware run sends the CC).
