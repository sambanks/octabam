# OCTA303 SEAM — the hybrid OCTA-303

The ColdFire keeps the knobs, the sequencer, the envelopes, the accent, the slide and the VCO; the DSP renders the
rest of the voice at the per-track source seam, before the stock AMP, FX1 and FX2: dEVILish's FM (the cutoff moved
per sample by the VCA's word), the resonance loop with its high-pass, the ladder clip, the four-stage ladder, the VCA
input pair, the level, the OTA, the VCA, pin 6, the Muffler (MUFF SOFT/HARD), the DIST pedal (the +D waves), the
output coupling and DRIV. Every voice plays here, FM, MUFF, DIST and DRIV included.

- **The seam** is ANALOG BD's: the stock `move a,x:>$20e` (payload A `P:0x39c`, B `P:0x1a2`) is a jsr into
  `o3h.asm`. A record with the signature renders into `X:0` and continues after the source (A `P:0x426`, B
  `P:0x221`); any other record returns into the stock source untouched.
- **The record** (octa303.s, `O3_SEAM`): format in `tools/octa303/ibrido3/hyb.py`. Each piece carries its control
  words, the feature flags (FM, MUFF, DIST, ACID, KEEP) and DRIV's gain; with FM, the piece carries the cutoff, the
  resonance law and FM's depth instead of the ladder's coefficients, and the DSP looks them up per sample.
- **FM's source is smoothed** (in the voice itself, `ref303.py` and `octa303.s` too): the VCA's word through a
  one-pole of 1/4 a sample (~2 kHz) before it moves the cutoff. At high FM with high RES and ENV the loop went
  chaotic ("TV static"); with the one-pole it stays wild but pitched — MaMarko's choice in a blind listening test.
- **`o3h.asm`** is generated by `tools/octa303/ibrido3/gen_o3h.py`: integer-exact against `ref303`, only
  instruction forms with a stock site or a CHIP.md register row (`forms2.py`: 128 forms, 0 without either). Its
  tables (pin 6; per ladder index LB LK KF KD for FM; the clip's; DRIV's tanh and constants; 2,755 words) are the
  section's ptable, parked in the stock curve bank when DJ EQ is out of the remix.
- **Memory**: X `$7c00..$7cff`, four blocks of 64 words (a voice's 32 state words, then its control block, all read
  through r7), in stock's unreferenced `X:$7a92-$7fff`.

- **A record whose sizes do not add up**: `start` plus the pieces' `n` must be 16; any other record renders
  32 zeros and continues after the source, so a damaged record cannot write past `X:0..31`.

## What checks it

| check | what it proves |
|---|---|
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/o3hhost.cpp` (vectors from `gen_vec.py`), both payloads | the DSP half from the seam's jsr, frame by frame, sample for sample against `ref303` (the judge's notes and random runs, with dEVILish FM, MUFF, SWP, DIST and DRIV), stock records returned untouched, damaged records refused with only X:0..31 written |
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/test_split.py` | `split303` (the model of the cut) and the record path against `ref303`; every multiplier operand below 2^23 |
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/test_seam_cf.py` | the ColdFire half in cfsim: every record equal to `hyb.py`'s, every frame's sound equal to `ref303` (FM, MUFF and DRIV switched within notes too) |
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/placed.py` | the image's placed words equal `o3h.asm` assembled there, the table and the jsr in place |
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/port_ab.py` | the same project under the port, the voice on the ColdFire against the hybrid: main out equal at a constant lag |
| `tools/octa303/ibrido3/forms2.py` | every instruction form has a stock site or a CHIP.md register row |

Cycles a sample, one voice (`interlock_scan.py`, a model from the manual, not the unit): plain 483, FM 584,
MUFF 599, DIST 566, DRIV 534, all four 824.

## Known limits

- **`make cycles` does not count it** (hook sections are not in the figure). A plain voice costs about as much as
  DISTORX + FET76 together on that core; one with FM, MUFF, DIST and DRIV all on ~1.7 times that. Put one hybrid
  303 in T1–T4 and one in T5–T8.
- **It needs the stock curve bank**: a remix with OCTA303 SEAM leaves DJ EQ out, so the tables (2,755 words, and
  the other modules' P tables) are parked in X:$4840.. and read with `x:(`.
- **It takes ANALOG BD's seam**: the two cannot share an image.
